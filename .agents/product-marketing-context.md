## Product overview

**Working description:** Eldur Studio develops off-the-shelf AI agents for specific jobs, with guidance that helps non-technical buyers use them.

**Primary business direction:** Reusable products and productized services that can serve multiple customers without a new custom implementation for each sale.

**Priority product:** Watchtower, an agent-assisted Webflow maintenance and change-request service. The linked briefing describes it as the priority product bet for recurring revenue.

**Other current offer:** The homepage presents the free B2B Messaging Workshop plugin, which guides a user through product messaging and produces a reusable messaging document.

**Consulting:** A secondary offer. Veronica has not said it is discontinued. Do not remove it or invent a new consulting scope or price without a separate decision.

**Important distinction:** Self-service describes access and ease of getting started. It does not mean every product is fully autonomous, requires no permissions, or has no human support. Describe setup, approvals, and ongoing delivery accurately for each product.

## Audience and jobs

**Company-wide audience:** Non-technical buyers who want an agent for a defined job with less implementation effort. Industry, company size, and budget should be qualified per product; there is no newly confirmed universal ICP.

**Watchtower customer:** A non-technical small-business owner with an existing Webflow site that is neglected, template-built, or left behind after a freelancer or agency handoff. They want ongoing care and changes without needing to operate Webflow Designer themselves.

**Messaging Workshop user:** Someone working through product messaging using the Codex plugin. The current homepage presents the offer as free. Do not assume the Watchtower customer profile also describes this user.

**Jobs to communicate:**

- Understand what a specific agent does and whether it fits the job.
- Start using it through a defined setup or onboarding process.
- Know what is included, what needs approval, and where human help is available.

## Problems and productization rationale

The productization source identifies heavy upfront development, time, commitment, and implementation effort as obstacles to selling custom agents. The intended direction is a narrowly scoped offer that is easy to explain and usable by a non-technical person with clear guidance.

For Watchtower, explain the concrete maintenance problem: a business has a Webflow site that needs care, but its owner does not want to manage the Designer or investigate every defect.

Do not force every product into a funnel-audit, enterprise CRM, or department-replacement story.

## Positioning and differentiation

Lead with the available product and the job it performs. Explain fit, deliverables, setup, price, ownership, support, and approvals where they affect the purchase.

Reusable product delivery and a smaller initial commitment are the strategic direction. Do not claim instant setup, zero implementation, guaranteed results, or universal suitability without evidence.

Watchtower is a productized, agent-assisted service. The old promise that every buyer receives a custom-built system and runs it independently after handoff does not describe every current offer.

Competitive comparisons can remain direct when they compare relevant, supported facts. Removing every comparison or softening the brand is not a requirement of the voice skill.

## Brand voice

The [Notion Brand Voice Skill](https://app.notion.com/p/31b61616825380a1b146e24f0cf2e588) is the governing voice guide. Consult its linked voice guidelines when a channel convention is needed and its messaging references when making positioning claims, while applying the updated product direction above.

- Write Eldur Studio in full.
- Use a professional, direct, warm, practitioner-led voice.
- Preserve the author’s meaning, judgment, and useful technical precision.
- Prefer specific actions, deliverables, and constraints over hype or abstract filler.
- Avoid em dashes, manufactured hooks, slogan-like oppositions, and false binaries.
- Vary sentence length naturally. Clarity does not require uniformly short sentences.
- Make consequential editorial choices explicit; distinguish copyediting from a strategy change.
