FAQ

Do you replace our agency or internal team?

Agents can take on work you currently give to an agency or an internal team. We start by working alongside them, then teach your team to run the agents. You still need someone who knows the business to set priorities and review the work.

What tools do you work with?

We often work with Notion, GA4, HubSpot, Salesforce, Google Ads, Meta, and content management systems. We start with your existing tools and recommend a change when it would help the work or make results easier to measure.

How is this different from…
  • A traditional marketing agency? We build agents around your marketing and sales processes, connect them to your tools, and teach your team to run them after the project ends.
  • Hiring a channel marketer? An agent can take on recurring tasks such as checking ad spend or qualifying leads. Your team still decides what matters and reviews the results.
  • Buying a marketing platform with "AI built in"? We build around your business rules, data, and existing tools. The scope depends on the job you need done.
  • A rev ops consultant or fractional CMO? We also do the work. Our scope includes building and connecting the agents, testing them with your team, and training you to use them.
What does pricing look like?

You can start with a first agent to try one workflow before commissioning a larger system.

We agree on the scope and fixed fee before the build. Each custom build includes training so your team can run the agents.

What do you need from us?

We need access to the tools we’re connecting and someone on your team who can make decisions. Plan for 30–60 minutes each week to review the work with us.

Will this break our existing systems?

We test changes before rolling them out and show you what changed. We’ll discuss any work that could affect existing systems before we start.

How do we start?

We’ll discuss the job you want to automate, the tools it needs, and what a first build would involve. Schedule a call to get started.
